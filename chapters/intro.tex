development.

- Macros are everywhere

In the domain of formal proof automation is everywhere, and it is formally
called elaboration~\cref{enrico}

In formal proof, proofs are verbose and have lot of details: types, coercions,
canonical structures, type-classes. 

In theorem provers the user benefit of notations, implicit inference and
tactics to ease his job.
